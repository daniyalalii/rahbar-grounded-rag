## Fee Structure

The following are the official fee structures for FAST-NUCES students for the year 2026.

## Admission Fees

| Program | Admission Fee |
|---------|--------------|
| BBA / BS | Rs. 12,000 |
| MS / MBA | Rs. 15,000 |
| PhD | Rs. 18,000 |

## Semester Fees

The semester fee is charged at the beginning of each semester. Late payment will result in a fine.

| Program | Per Semester |
|---------|-------------|
| BS (Engineering) | Rs. 55,000 |
| BS (Computer Science) | Rs. 50,000 |
| BBA | Rs. 45,000 |

## Miscellaneous Fees

| Service | Fee |
|---------|-----|
| Additional Transcript | Rs. 300 |
| Duplicate Degree | Rs. 2,000 |
| Campus Transfer | Rs. 5,000 |
| Late Registration / Freeze (after 2nd week) | Rs. 5,000 |
| Re-admission | Rs. 5,000 |

Fees are subject to revision without prior notice. Students are advised to check the official portal regularly.
