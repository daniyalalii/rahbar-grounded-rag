## Code of Conduct

All students at FAST-NUCES are expected to uphold the highest standards of academic integrity
and personal conduct. Violations may result in disciplinary action ranging from a formal warning
to dismissal from the university.

## Academic Integrity

Plagiarism, cheating, or any form of academic dishonesty is strictly prohibited. All submitted
work must be the student's own. Collaboration is permitted only where explicitly allowed by
the instructor.

## Disciplinary Process

Cases of misconduct are reviewed by the Disciplinary Committee. Students have the right to
present their case before the committee. The committee's decision may be appealed to the Dean.

## Conduct Outside Campus

Students represent the university in public and are expected to behave accordingly. Conduct
that brings disrepute to the university may be subject to disciplinary action even if it
occurs off campus.
