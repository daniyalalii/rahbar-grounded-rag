## University Fee Policy

The fee schedule below applies to all FAST-NUCES students enrolled in the 2026 academic year.

## Admission Charges

| Degree Program | Admission Fee |
|----------------|--------------|
| BBA / BS Programs | Rs. 12,000 |
| MS / MBA Programs | Rs. 15,000 |
| PhD Programs | Rs. 18,000 |

## Per-Semester Tuition

Tuition is due at the start of each semester. Delayed payment incurs a penalty.

| Degree Level | Fee Per Semester |
|--------------|-----------------|
| BS Engineering | Rs. 55,000 |
| BS Computer Science | Rs. 50,000 |
| Bachelor of Business Administration | Rs. 45,000 |

## Other Charges

| Service Description | Charge |
|--------------------|--------|
| Transcript (additional copy) | Rs. 300 |
| Degree (duplicate) | Rs. 2,000 |
| Transfer between campuses | Rs. 5,000 |
| Late Registration after week 2 | Rs. 5,000 |
| Re-admission processing | Rs. 5,000 |

All fees are subject to periodic revision. Please consult the student portal for updated information.
