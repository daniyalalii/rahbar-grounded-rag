## Graduate Program Requirements

Graduate students (MS and PhD) at FAST-NUCES must meet specific academic requirements in addition
to those applicable to undergraduate students.

## MS Program Requirements

MS students must complete a minimum of 30 credit hours, including a thesis or a project component
worth 6 credit hours. The thesis must be defended before a committee of at least three members.

## PhD Program Requirements

PhD candidates must pass a comprehensive examination before being admitted to candidacy. The
dissertation must represent an original contribution to knowledge and must be defended publicly.
PhD students must complete a minimum of 48 credit hours beyond the BS degree.

## Thesis Submission

The thesis/dissertation must be submitted to the graduate office at least 30 days before the
intended defense date. The graduate office will distribute copies to committee members.

## Research Ethics

All research involving human subjects must receive approval from the Institutional Review Board
before data collection begins. Data must be stored securely and retained for a minimum of 5 years.
