## Library Rules

Students must return borrowed books within 14 days. Overdue books incur a fine of Rs. 10 per day.
Quiet zones are strictly enforced in reading rooms. Mobile phones must be on silent mode.
Students may borrow up to 3 books at a time with a valid student ID.
