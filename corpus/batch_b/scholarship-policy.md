## Scholarship Programs

FAST-NUCES offers several merit-based and need-based scholarship programs to support deserving students.

## Merit Scholarships

Students who secure positions in the top 5% of their batch in any semester are eligible for a
25% fee waiver in the following semester. The CGPA must be 3.5 or above.

Students who top their entire batch in a semester receive a 50% fee waiver for the following semester.

## Need-Based Financial Aid

Students facing genuine financial hardship may apply for need-based aid. Applications must be
submitted with supporting documentation (family income certificate, utility bills, etc.) to the
Financial Aid Office within the first two weeks of each semester.

## Scholarship Revocation

Scholarships may be revoked if the student's CGPA drops below 3.0, the student is placed on
academic probation, or the student is found guilty of academic misconduct.
