## Academic Warning Policy 2026

Under the updated 2026 rules, students who score below 2.0 CGPA are placed on formal academic probation.
Students on probation cannot register for more than 15 credit hours in the following term.

## Course Repeat Improvement 2026

In the 2026 policy, students may repeat any course with a grade below C. The improved grade
replaces the old grade in the CGPA calculation, though the original attempt is retained for records.
