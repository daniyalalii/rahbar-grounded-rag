## Academic Policies Overview

FAST-NUCES maintains high academic standards across all programs. Students are expected to adhere
to the policies outlined in this handbook throughout their academic journey.

## Attendance Policy

Students must maintain a minimum of 80% attendance in every course to be eligible to sit for the
final examination. Attendance is recorded by the respective faculty member each session.

## Grade Improvement

Students who wish to improve their grade may repeat a course. Only the higher grade will be
considered in the CGPA calculation. A course may be repeated a maximum of two times.

## Academic Calendar

The academic year consists of two regular semesters (Fall and Spring) and an optional Summer semester.
Each semester spans approximately 18 weeks including examination periods.
