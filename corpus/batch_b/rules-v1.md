## Academic Warning Policy 2023

Under the 2023 rules, students who score below 2.0 CGPA receive a warning letter from the Dean.
Students on warning must meet their academic advisor weekly.

## Course Repeat Improvement 2023

In the 2023 policy, course repeats are limited to 3 courses total across the 4-year degree.
The previous grade remains on the student transcript with an asterisk notation.
